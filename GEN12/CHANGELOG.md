# Changelog

## v1.1.0 — GEN-12 expansion and balance

- Added the 7.25-inch PDW barrel with its own 3D bundle and a working inventory preview, retaining the established barrel item ID.
- Added the 7-inch SBS7 M-LOK handguard in **standard** and **SOCOM-enhanced** variants, with a shared front-sight/tactical-device slot.
- Added the GEN-12-compatible **Genesis-12 Suppressor**.
- Updated all three SOCOM TTI Dracarys KeyMod-style handguards (JW4 Grey, Black, FDE) to **-14% recoil** (previously -6%).
- Updated the SOCOM SBS7 handguard to **-9% recoil** and **+19 ergonomics** (previously -6% and +14).
- Kept the standard SBS7 at **-1% recoil** and **+4 ergonomics**.
- Preserved established item IDs, compatibility filters, prices, and SOCOM LL3 trader availability.
- Cleaned English item descriptions and removed experimental phrasing from the PDW barrel's Portuguese and Russian descriptions.
- Removed historical test files and organized the distribution into the DLL, two Unity bundles, item definitions, trader offers, and documentation.

**Validation:** The GEN-12 attachments, barrel preview, and final balance changes passed in-game testing on the prior release-preparation build. The v1.1.0 DLL and distribution contents were structurally verified against the matching v1.1.0 source.
